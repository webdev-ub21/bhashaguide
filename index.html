BhashaGuard Architecture & Explainable AI Methodology

How our 6-layer Indic linguistic engine operates, why AI detection is probabilistic, and our protocols for avoiding false accusations.

Layer 1: Language & Script ID

Unicode block mapping + n-gram script detection. Identifies Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gurmukhi, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Gujarati, Urdu, and Romanized Indian code-switching.

Layer 2: Rule-Based Indic Linguistics

Examines postpositions, Karaka structures, Tatsam/Tadbhav ratios, Anusvara/Anunasika distribution, and Samyukt-akshar density instead of blindly imposing English grammar models.

Layer 3: Statistical Burstiness & TTR

Calculates sentence-length variance (σ), clause symmetry, and root Type-Token Ratio. Machine generation displays unnaturally uniform sentence cadence.

Layer 4: Formulaic Indian Discourse

Detects overused LLM translation crutches (e.g., "यह ध्यान देना महत्वपूर्ण है कि...", "निष्कर्षतः...", "इसके अतिरिक्त...") without penalizing genuine human usage.

Layer 5: LLM Contextual Reasoning

Leverages Gemini 3 Flash as an evaluator to assess semantic depth, local Indian context, and contextual colloquialism.

Layer 6: Ensemble Decision Calibration

Fuses signals into a probabilistic range (0–100%). Scores below 100 words receive an automatic "Insufficient text" disclaimer to protect academic integrity.

Critical False Positive Notice for Evaluators & Universities

Standardized academic prose, government policy circulars, legal affidavits, and scientific definitions naturally exhibit high grammatical regularity and structured discourse markers. Never penalize a student, researcher, or employee based on automated scores alone. Always conduct a human interview and review document version history.
